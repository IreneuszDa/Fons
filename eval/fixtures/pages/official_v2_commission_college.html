Members of the von der Leyen Commission

The College is composed of Commissioners from 27 EU countries. Together, these 27 members of the College are appointed as the Commission's political leadership for a 5-year term.

The President

Ursula von der Leyen

  • Raffaele Fitto — Cohesion and Reforms
